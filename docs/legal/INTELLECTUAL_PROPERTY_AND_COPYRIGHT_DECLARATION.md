## 4. Scope of Rights Covered

Different kinds of intellectual property protect different things and
arise (or must be secured) differently. This declaration covers all of
them; it does not, by itself, complete registration for any of them.

- **Copyright** — the source code, documentation, and creative/product
  expression described in Section 2. In most jurisdictions, including
  those following the Berne Convention, copyright arises automatically
  upon creation and does not require registration to exist — but
  registration materially strengthens the ability to enforce it and to
  prove authorship and priority.
- **Trademark** — the "LUZ" name and associated branding. Unlike
  copyright, meaningful trademark protection generally requires
  registration or demonstrable use in commerce in each relevant
  jurisdiction; this declaration records intent and claimed ownership,
  it does not itself constitute a trademark registration.
- **Trade secrets** — proprietary architecture decisions, prompts,
  data models, and business logic not publicly disclosed. Protected
  through confidentiality and access control, not registration; this
  declaration is not itself a confidentiality agreement.

## 5. Relationship to AI-Assisted Development

This declaration does not modify `AI_DEVELOPMENT_POLICY.md` — it
relies on it. That policy already establishes that AI participation in
LUZ's development is accelerative and tool-like, never authorial, and
that human review and acceptance govern what becomes part of the
product (`AI_DEVELOPMENT_POLICY.md`, Sections 3–5). This declaration
draws the ownership conclusion that follows from that governance
model.

This area carries real, jurisdiction-specific legal nuance that this
document does not resolve: several copyright offices (notably the
U.S. Copyright Office) evaluate the degree and nature of human
creative control when determining whether — and how much of — an
AI-assisted work is copyrightable, and may require disclosure of AI
involvement at the time of formal registration. Before pursuing formal
copyright or trademark registration for LUZ, this specific question
should be reviewed with qualified counsel, informed by the actual
development record this project already keeps (Git history, ADRs,
`AI_DEVELOPMENT_POLICY.md`, sprint documentation).

## 7. Future Contributors and Changes to Ownership

Ownership as declared here reflects the project's current state — a
sole founder and developer. It is expected to require deliberate
updates, not silent drift, if any of the following occur:

- A co-founder, employee, or contractor begins contributing IP to
  LUZ — their contribution should be governed by a written agreement
  (employment or contractor agreement with an IP assignment clause)
  before, not after, the contribution is made.
- A legal entity (company) is incorporated to hold LUZ — ownership
  does not transfer automatically; it requires an explicit, written IP
  assignment from Juan David Pinzón, personally, to that entity.
- An investor, partner, or licensee is granted any right in LUZ's
  intellectual property — that grant should be documented separately
  and referenced here, not left implicit.

Per [`docs/governance/DOCUMENT_CLASSIFICATION.md`](../governance/DOCUMENT_CLASSIFICATION.md),
this document is Normative: it is updated directly when the underlying
reality changes, rather than superseded through an approval workflow.
